It is not a public SaaS offering, and KrakenTic is not yet identified as a registered legal company.

Authorization and permissionsالتفويض والصلاحيات

You must have authority to connect the Google account and process the content selected for each task. You are responsible for reviewing the permissions requested on Google's consent screen and any authorized automated workflows.

The Connector requests broad Google permissions, described in the Privacy Policy; grant only access you are prepared to have the agent use. You can revoke Google access through your Google Account. Revocation may interrupt connected features.

Permitted use and restrictionsالاستخدام المسموح والقيود

The Connector is intended to assist with legitimate, authorized personal or internal business productivity tasks, such as organizing information, preparing drafts, and coordinating schedules.

Do not use it to access accounts without permission, scrape data, send unsolicited bulk messages, disclose confidential information without authorization, evade Google policies, or process regulated or highly sensitive personal records without appropriate safeguards and explicit approval.

AI-generated results and automated actionsنتائج الذكاء الاصطناعي والإجراءات الآلية

Where tools are configured to perform actions, such actions may affect email, files, or calendar data. The operator has not verified that every consequential action, such as sending an email, sharing a file, or deleting a file or calendar event, requires explicit human approval before execution; do not assume that the agent will always ask first. Use such tools only with clear authorization and suitable oversight. The current pilot should not be used with real customer or patient data until relevant controls have been verified.

Privacy and external servicesالخصوصية والخدمات الخارجية

Information handled through the Connector is described in our Privacy Policy. The Connector currently sends relevant task content to OpenAI for AI processing; whether OpenAI uses that content for model training, and how long it retains it, has not been verified. Session history, logs and caches on the server may contain Google-derived content, and no verified automatic deletion schedule exists. Retention and deletion controls are still being reviewed as part of private development.

Use of connected Google services is also subject to Google's own terms, account permissions, and applicable API policies.
